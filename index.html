About Will

Will Ashley Ramos de Leon is a Filipino actor and singer under GMA Network’s Sparkle. After about twelve years in showbiz, 2026 has become a breakthrough year. He won Best Supporting Actor at the EDDYS and the Gawad Urian for Bar Boys: After School, and released his debut EP, Liham.
